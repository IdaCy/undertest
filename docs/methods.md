# Methods and interpretation

Keep the assignment frame, source identity, protocol version and measurement profile with every result. Cues and experimental interventions, actor behavior, expressed reasoning, self-reports and reader judgments are separate variables. Task success alone does not identify metagaming. Reader agreement measures an instrument property; it is distinct from coherence of a model's behavior or beliefs.

Preserve false, invalid, unavailable, unissued, interrupted and unscorable outcomes as defined by each protocol. Missing outcomes remain in assigned denominators. Identification bounds over possible missing outcomes are distinct from sampling intervals. Observed-case summaries must identify the observations on which they condition.

Cue-factor kernels retain paired exact tests, worst-completion treatment of missing pairs, and Holm adjustment for the declared family. Their confidence calculations depend on the stated independence or exchangeability assumptions. The layout-label analysis retains 720 assigned calls, 240 matched triples and exactly two primary contrasts. Its subgroup tables are descriptive. Binary and graded signal instruments have separate schemas; their crosswalk is a descriptive comparison of measurement packages.

The repeated-account calculation retains 56 original assignments, 28 actor/task pairs and the original pair-weight rule. Reader/question reuse does not multiply the independent pair count. Both-null actions and both-missing labels are separate states. Equality of recorded labels does not establish equality of private beliefs.

Full-continuation projection requires callers to authenticate closed actor records first. It preserves 24 assigned positions per model, six paired tasks, F07/F08 endpoints, scoped native-check outcomes and missing clocks. Raw checker success is not substituted for unavailable scoped success. Actor elapsed time is distinct from later checker or evidence-collection time.

Checkpoint awareness V1 and V3 retain different coverage rules. V3 checks original packet bytes, literal spans, temporal/support/retraction constraints and annotation coverage. Passing the validator establishes structural consistency of supplied annotations; semantic accuracy requires calibration. Synthetic source fixtures never become evidence of an actual model run.

Training reward parsers are versioned. `emergence.training.rewards.terminal_legacy` preserves the earlier extraction contract; `terminal_v5` contains the later terminal-region correction. The legacy teacher-bank contract explicitly imports the legacy parser. Selecting the correction changes reward semantics and does not make earlier scored states or checkpoints compatible. Numerical and schedule kernels retain their own protocol limits, and the optional tensor kernel is an implementation candidate rather than a claim of GPU equivalence.
