# Project overview

Undertest studies metagaming through four related questions. Causes concerns cues, triggers, context and interventions. Characteristics concerns reappearance under resampling, coherence and contradictions, continuations, sensitivity and downstream behavior. Signals concerns the evidence available from reasoning, self-reports and independently measured behavior. Emergence concerns released training checkpoints and controlled comparisons between training methods.

A study has one source home and may inform several questions. A second analysis of the same actors or episodes is another view of those experimental units. It does not create another set of independent observations. Reusable measurements live under signals and are imported by the other areas.

The [machine-readable catalogue](../src/studies.json) lists implemented interfaces. The recipe/control and report studies include runnable tasks and saved-result reconciliation. Checkpoint calibration includes panel preparation, execution and historical result readers. Signal controls and continuations include their existing frozen-design interfaces. Later cue-factor, layout-label, resampling, repeated-account, full-continuation and training work currently exposes the portable calculations and contracts described in the catalogue.

Training code includes matched-data preparation, exact scalar rewards, explicit legacy and corrected terminal extraction, group validation, saved release reading, audit bounds, schedule geometry and numerical components. It does not expose an end-to-end multi-host training campaign. The retained 2048-record, 512-slot schedule is an explicitly versioned historical protocol component; its presence is not a claim that it is the current accepted campaign schedule or that a campaign completed.

This source snapshot makes no new claim about accepted model outcomes, causal effects or completed training runs. [Methods](methods.md) explains the interpretation boundaries; [reproduction](reproduction.md) distinguishes local checks from model execution.
